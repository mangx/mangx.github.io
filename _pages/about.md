About me
======
I am currently a third-year master's student at the Graduate School of the Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences (CAAS), affiliated with the Institute of Agricultural Information of CAAS. Specifically, I carry out my study and research activities in the China National Agricultural Science Data Center.

I have currently completed my master's studies in Management Science and Engineering and obtained a Master of Management degree. Previously, I finished my undergraduate studies in Information Management and Information Systems, earning a Bachelor of Management degree. My current research mainly focuses on the interdisciplinary field of management and computer science, making me an academic amphibian who alternates between "Taylor's scientific management" and "Taylor expansion".

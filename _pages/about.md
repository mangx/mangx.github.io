About me
======
I am currently a Ph.D. student at the **University of Toyama**, under the supervision of **Prof. Chao Zhang**. My primary research focuses on **3D skeleton-based action recognition**, with a specific emphasis on **dataset distillation** to optimize training efficiency for general downstream tasks. Recently, I have extended my research into **fine-grained emotion recognition**, leveraging skeleton sequences to distinguish complex social emotions through advanced deep learning architectures.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I spent three years as a Master's student at the **National Agricultural Science Data Center (NASDC)** of the Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences. During this period, my work centered on the **processing, mining, and application of complex agricultural big data**. I focused on enhancing data utilization rates by integrating computer science techniques—such as **Large Language Models (LLMs)** and **Knowledge Graphs**—into the agricultural domain. My experience involved developing automated systems for information extraction and decision support, aiming to bridge the gap between massive raw data and practical agricultural insights.
